About Me

I am an applied economist working on topics in labor economics, immigration and education. My recent work combines microeconometric analysis and field experiments to better understand how to support jobseekers.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Labor Group led by Rafael Lalive at the University of Lausanne. I completed my PhD in Economics at the University of St.Gallen (2022). I visited the NHH in Bergen in 2024, hosted by and the Universitat Pompeu Fabra in 2021 and 2022, hosted by Albrecht Glitz.

Research

Job Search and Cognitive Skills

Exploring how cognitive training and personalized job recommendations can enhance job search outcomes among unemployed workers.

Mental Health During Global Crises

Using helpline data to analyze how policy responses and social conditions affect well-being and distress in times of crisis.

Labor Market Matching in the Digital Age

Investigating how digital tools and AI-based platforms can improve job matching and employment transitions.
